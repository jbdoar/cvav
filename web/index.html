What’s happening here?

Inspired by J. P. Crutchfield’s 1984 video-feedback models: each image combines the last frame, a softened copy, a rotated and scaled copy, and new input. Color channels can feed into one another. Clipping keeps the signal bounded. These are digital approximations of a camera looking at its own monitor.

Processing runs locally on your graphics hardware. The loop targets 30 iterations per second and stops while this tab is hidden. Use headphones for your own soundtrack; this experiment doesn’t request microphone access.
